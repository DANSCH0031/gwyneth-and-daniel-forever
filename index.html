04FAQs

Good questions

If your question isn't answered here, just ask us.

Please let us know by 1 June 2027 using the RSVP form. If your plans change later, just send us a message.

We have reserved rooms for our guests at the Inselhotel Faaker See, where we'll celebrate after the ceremony. Book through the hotel's website and use this code:

GWENANDDANIEL

Rooms are limited, so we recommend booking early. There are also plenty of hotels and holiday apartments around Faaker See and Villach.

The nearest airport is Klagenfurt. Ljubljana, Salzburg and Venice are also a few hours' drive away, and Villach is the closest major train station. On the day, we'll provide a shuttle between Sternberg and the Inselhotel, so you won't need to drive. We'll share the shuttle times here closer to the date.

Festive and elegant: a suit, a summer dress or a jumpsuit is perfect. Part of the day is outdoors on grass and by the lake, so choose shoes you can walk in comfortably, and bring a light jacket for the evening.

Always! Children are very welcome. Please include them in your guest count when you RSVP so we can plan seats and meals.

Your invitation lists everyone it includes. If you're not sure, just ask us. We're happy to help.

No gifts, please. Having you there to celebrate with us is the best gift we could ask for.

Mid-September in Carinthia is usually lovely: warm, sunny days, often around 20–25 °C, with cooler evenings. A light layer for after sunset is a good idea.
